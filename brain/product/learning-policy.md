# Learning Policy (product notes)

From PRD P1: remove logistical friction, preserve productive learning
friction.

- Automate ingestion, organization, concept extraction, scheduling, and
  practice selection.
- Do not auto-reveal answers when retrieval or a small hint would be more
  pedagogically valuable — this is a deliberate friction to preserve.
- The review scheduler produces both a short daily session and a longer
  exam-prep session (see PRD MVP goal 13).
- Practice selection should favor retrieval, spacing, and interleaving over
  passive restudy, per the learning-science grounding cited in the PRD.
